Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 September 2026

This policy covers the information at exitleftshows.com, operated by ExitLeft Productions Pty Ltd. ExitLeft is a performing arts company based in Hobart, Tasmania.

What we collect on this page

This site does not collect personal information directly. We use the Meta Pixel to measure ad performance (anonymous page views and clicks). No name, email, or payment data is collected here.

Where bookings happen

Ticked bookings purchased via the Playhouse Theatre box office are processed by TicketSearch ticketing portal. When you click Book Now you will be taken to TicketSearch's secure checkout, and any personal or payment information you provide is handled under their privacy policy. See: ticketsearch.com/au/privacy.

Meta advertising

We run paid ads on Facebook and Instagram. The Meta Pixel on these pages (ID 2434667176671774) tells Meta when an ad click reaches the page so we can measure performance and retarget visitors. You can manage Meta's ad personalisation at facebook.com/settings?tab=ads.

Hosting

This page is hosted by Vercel Inc. Server logs (IP address, browser type, time of request) are retained briefly for security and performance monitoring.

Contact

Questions can be directed to the Exitleft Team at info@exitleft.com.au or 0408 145 898.
