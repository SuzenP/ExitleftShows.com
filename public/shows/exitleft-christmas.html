About the show

packed with music, laughter and a whole
lot of festive cheer!

From festive favourites and Christmas classics to modern holiday hits, our performers will fill the theatre with soaring vocals, joyful harmonies, big performances and plenty of Christmas spirit.

Whether you're looking for a little extra Christmas magic, a festive family outing, or simply an excuse to sing along to your favourite Christmas songs, A Very ExitLeft Christmas is the perfect way to kick off the holiday season.

When booking, please check that you have selected the correct concert time:

Monday 21 December, 5pm & 7pm
Performed by Monday and Thursday (Gr3-12) Accelerate and Blaze Performers (Runtime: approximately 75 minutes with no interval. All Accelerate and Blaze performers will be appearing in both sessions.)

Tuesday 22 December, 5pm & 7pm
Performed by Tuesday and Friday (Gr3-12) Accelerate and Blaze Performers (Runtime: approximately 75 minutes with no interval. All Accelerate and Blaze performers will be appearing in both sessions.)

Wednesday 23 December, 5pm & 6:15pm
Performed by all (K-2) Ignite Performers (Runtime: approximately 40 minutes with no interval. All Ignite performers will be appearing in both sessions.)

The concert is suitable for ages 2+.

All persons entering the theatre, regardless of age, must have a ticket. Even though our amplified sound is at comfortable levels for young people and adults, for health and safety reasons we advise that this performance is not suitable for children under 2 years.
