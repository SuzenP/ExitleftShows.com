About the show

Wildly funny. Wildly magical.
The global hit comedy for anyone who never got a letter!

Puffs is the hilarious and affectionate unofficial parody that has taken theatres around the world by storm. Packed with clever references, outrageous comedy, ridiculous characters and more than a little magic, it's a celebration of the stories we grew up with, told from the perspective of the kids who were never at the centre of them.

You don't need to know every spell, character or detail of the wizarding world to love Puffs. But if you do? You're going to LOVE these friendly, loyal, quirky and long-suffering misfits whose heartfelt journey takes the classic story to new places!
